App icon

A pixel fish outline in aqua on a black square, drawn on a 16 by 16 grid and scaled up without smoothing.

Loading fish

Food drops in, the fish swims over and eats it, then swims back to the middle. It repeats until loading is done, then the fish flips and the tank fades out.

Download bar

The fish eats the dots as the download moves forward. These are the five states it can be in.

Downloading

Chomps a dot each time progress passes one.

Paused

Stops and blows bubbles.

Checking files

Swims back over the finished part to verify it.

Error

Turns gray and belly up, with a Retry button.

Done

Jumps out with a splash, then the bar becomes Play.
